Pedagogical notes
Output spatial size formula:
H_out = ⌊(H + 2P − K) / S⌋ + 1
(here K=3, square input/output)

At each step: take the 3×3 receptive field, multiply elementwise by weights, sum, then optionally apply ReLU.
